Memory system

Review before you forget.

StudyTrack automatically creates a review timetable instead of asking you to remember when to revise.

StudyTrack spaced repetition schedule

Every new topic gets multiple future reviews. The default schedule deliberately increases the interval between reviews.

Review 1 — Tomorrow
Confirm that you still remember the main idea.
Review 2 — 2 days later
Recall the concept without looking at your notes first.
Review 3 — 4 days later
Test yourself with questions or practice problems.
Review 4 — 7 days later
Reconstruct the topic from memory.
Review 5 — 14 days later
Check long-term retention.
Review 6 — 30 days later
Final long-term reinforcement.

Leitner method

Use a second learning system for flashcards. Cards move into increasingly difficult boxes when you answer correctly.

Box 2 Every 2 days
